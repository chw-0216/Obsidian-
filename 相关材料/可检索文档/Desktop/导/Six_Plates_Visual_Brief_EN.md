# 1. GLOBAL DIRECTION

**Title:** JINGLIN | CO-PRINTING A VILLAGE
(original: 井邻｜共印一座村庄)

**Place:** A rural art-education workstation in Dongtai Village, Bohaizhen,
Huairou District, Beijing. Real elements: grey brick walls, weathered wooden
tables, old timber doors, lattice windows, a small courtyard, branches and
leaves, prints hanging on a line, silk-screen frames and squeegees.

**Palette — strictly limited**

| Colour | Hex ref | Role |
|--------|---------|------|
| Warm paper white | #F2EDE3 | Background, paper grain visible |
| Deep forest green | #1F4A3C | Large Chinese serif type, main structure |
| Vermilion red | #D93A22 | Overprint blocks, focal accents |
| Ultramarine blue | #1F3A93 | Plate 06 only |
| Cadmium yellow | #E8B22B | Small accent, plate 04 only |

**Typography:** Large Chinese Song / Ming serif characters, confident weight.
Horizontal or vertical setting both acceptable.

**Visual language — must be present**
- Silk-screen / risograph / stencil-print feel
- Halftone dot patterns — never smooth continuous-tone gradients
- Slight misregistration: colour blocks offset by a hair, like hand-pulled layers
- Visible paper fibre and grain
- Large flat solid areas + generous empty paper
- Printmaking contrast, no realistic lighting or shadow

**Absolutely avoid**
- No Yunnan / Lancang content, no tropical or ethnic-minority motifs
- No photographic realism, no 3D, no glow, no lens flare
- No gradients, no soft shading, no watercolour blends
- No cartoon, no mascot, no clip-art
- No ornate decoration, no busy detail
- No extra text beyond the specified title

# 2. THE SIX PLATES (three two-colour pairs)

## GROUP ONE — The Workstation Space

### PLATE 01 — "The Door Opens"
```
Silkscreen poster artwork, A4 portrait.

Subject: an old timber door and a lattice window of a rural art
workstation in north China. The architecture is reduced to bold halftone
dot masses — door frame, lattice grid, brick wall simplified into flat
printed shapes. No realistic shading, dots only. A small circular
rainbow-arch logo sits in the bottom corner, kept tiny.

Palette: warm paper-white ground with deep forest green halftone.
Generous empty space around the subject.

Style: silkscreen print, risograph, halftone dots, limited palette,
paper texture, printmaking, high contrast, flat shapes,
A4 portrait, 300 dpi, no gradients.
```

### PLATE 02 — "Growth"  (overprint layer for Plate 01)
```
Overprint separation layer, A4 portrait, designed to be printed
on top of Plate 01.

Subject: branches and leaves pushing through the door opening and
the window lattice, then extending outward to a wooden table,
a silk-screen frame, and a hanging line with prints clipped to it.
Shapes are simplified and flat, no outlines, no detail.

Palette: VERMILION RED only — flat solid blocks on transparent/paper white.
Deliberate slight misregistration against Plate 01.
Visible paper grain and halftone edge texture.

Style: silkscreen separation layer, stencil print, flat colour,
A4 portrait, 300 dpi, no gradients, no shading.
```

## GROUP TWO — On-Site Printing

### PLATE 03 — "The Press Bed"
```
Silkscreen poster artwork, A4 portrait.

Subject: a screen-printing station seen from directly above —
mesh frame, rubber squeegee, ink tins, a worn wooden table top,
and a few freshly pulled prints lying to one side.
Everything rendered as deep-green halftone dots and flat solid shapes.

Palette: warm paper-white ground, deep forest green only.
Bold graphic simplification, silkscreen poster language.

Style: risograph, halftone dots, limited palette, paper texture,
high contrast, flat shapes, A4 portrait, 300 dpi, no gradients.
```

### PLATE 04 — "Co-Printing"  (overprint layer for Plate 03)
```
Overprint separation layer, A4 portrait, for printing on top of Plate 03.

Subject: two hands gripping and pulling a squeegee across the mesh.
A wide flat colour field spreads outward from the frame and connects
at the edges to simplified plant leaf shapes.

Palette: VERMILION RED as the main flat field, with a small
CADMIUM YELLOW accent block.
Slight misregistration, halftone edge texture, paper grain.

Style: silkscreen separation layer, flat colour blocks only,
A4 portrait, 300 dpi, no gradients, no shading, no outlines.
```

## GROUP THREE — Visual Exploration

### PLATE 05 — "Strata"
```
Silkscreen poster artwork, A4 portrait.

Subject: a human portrait dismantled into pure black paper-cut
silhouettes, contour outlines and halftone dot masses, stacked and
layered like sheets of paper building up into a face and profile.
Inspired by reduction woodcut / lost-plate printmaking.

Palette: warm paper-white ground, pure black only.
Extremely minimal, very high contrast, large areas of untouched paper.

Style: reduction printmaking, woodcut, halftone, silkscreen,
flat black shapes, A4 portrait, 300 dpi, no gradients, no grey shading.
```

### PLATE 06 — "Response"  (overprint layer for Plate 05)
```
Overprint separation layer, A4 portrait, for printing on top of Plate 05.

Subject: the trail of a hand gesture transforming into sound waves —
concentric rings, rippling arcs and offset contour lines —
wrapping around and overlaying the portrait beneath.

Palette: ULTRAMARINE BLUE only — flat shapes and thin clean lines.
Slight misregistration, paper texture.

Style: silkscreen separation layer, flat colour, technical yet organic,
A4 portrait, 300 dpi, no gradients, no glow, no shading.
```

# 4. HOW THE PLATES COMBINE

```
01 The Door Opens  ┐
                   ├─ Pair One → Work One  (the space)
02 Growth          ┘

03 The Press Bed   ┐
                   ├─ Pair Two → Work Two  (the making)
04 Co-Printing     ┘

05 Strata          ┐
                   ├─ Pair Three → Work Three  (the exploration)
06 Response        ┘
```

**Cross-pairing is allowed and encouraged** — e.g. Plate 01 (the door and
window) overprinted with Plate 06 (gesture sound-waves), letting the rural
site meet the digital response directly.

**Final object — a book you can see through:**
- The "site" plates (01 03 05) are printed on **opaque paper**
- The "response" plates (02 04 06) are printed on **translucent sheets**
- As you turn the pages: branches enter the room, hands enter the
  homestead, movement enters the portrait.
