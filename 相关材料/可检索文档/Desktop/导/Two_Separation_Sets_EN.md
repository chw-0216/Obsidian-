# PART A — JINGLIN PRINT SEPARATIONS
## 井邻印刷分版

### A.0 The master artwork
**Title:** JINGLIN | CO-PRINTING A VILLAGE  (井邻｜共印一座村庄)

**Scene:** A rural art-education workstation in Dongtai Village, Huairou,
Beijing. An old timber door and a lattice window; branches and leaves
pushing through the opening; a worn wooden table in front carrying a
silk-screen frame, a squeegee and fresh prints; a line of prints hanging
above. A small circular rainbow-arch logo tucked in one corner. A small
tag reading "悲鸿 / BEIHONG".

# PART B — ANIMAL ANIMATION SEPARATIONS
## 动物连贯成动画分版

### B.0 Concept
A sequence of frames of the **same animal in motion**, designed so that
viewing the frames in order produces continuous movement — a hand-flipped
animation. Each frame doubles as a **single-colour print separation**,
so the set can be printed, stacked and flipped.

**Frame count — choose one**

| Option | Frames | Effect | Plates |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Minimal | **2** | sharp, strobing flight — matches the "two drawings" reference | 2 |
| **Recommended** | **4** | readable wing-beat cycle, still easy to print | 4 |
| Smooth | **6** | near-continuous flight | 6 |

---

### B.1 Frame-strip preview prompt
*(generate first — shows the whole cycle at a glance)*
```
A vertical strip of four sequential animation frames of the SAME bird
in flight, arranged top to bottom on one sheet.

Each frame shows the same bird silhouette in a different phase of the
wing-beat cycle: wings fully raised, wings mid-stroke, wings fully
lowered, wings mid-stroke returning. The body drifts slightly upward
across the frames so the bird appears to climb.

Rendered as bold flat black shapes with coarse halftone dot masses,
on warm paper white. No outlines, no detail, no feathers — pure
silhouette and dot texture. Clear equal spacing between frames.

Style: silkscreen print, risograph, halftone dots, limited palette,
paper texture, high contrast, flat shapes, 300 dpi,
no gradients, no shading, no cartoon style.
```

---

---

### B.3 Two-colour option
If the animation is to be printed in two colours instead of one,
split each frame into:

| Plate | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Black plate** | the bird's body mass — solid black silhouette |
| **Vermilion plate** | the wing shapes only, as flat vermilion — offset a few mm to create the motion smear |

This produces a stronger sense of movement when flipped.
